Every set, logged honestly.

Goodrep is a strength-training log for iPhone. Build your workouts once, then log each set in a tap while your rest timer counts down.

  • No account
  • No ads
  • No tracking
  • Your own iCloud

01 / 06 · Pick

Tap a muscle.

Choose from more than 400 exercises by tapping a muscle group on a front-and-back body map, or create your own and add a photo.

02 / 06 · Plan

Build it once.

Set targets such as 3 × 8–10, pair exercises into supersets, split a long workout with headers and give each exercise its own rest time.

  • Targets
  • Supersets
  • Breaks
  • Timed

03 / 06 · Log

One tap a set.

Swipe from one exercise to the next. Weight and reps are filled in from last time, and your earlier sessions sit right under the fields, so you know what to beat. The rest timer can start by itself.

  • kg · lb · st
  • Rest timer
  • Spoken cue

04 / 06 · Finish

See what you moved.

The summary shows sets, reps, volume and time, plus a front-and-back map of the muscles you trained. Share it as an image.

05 / 06 · Look back

Every day on record.

A calendar marks every training day; open one to see each set you did. Charts follow your lifts, and the Body tab keeps your weight, body fat and tape measurements.

06 / 06 · Yours

Nobody else sees it.

No sign-up, no ads, no analytics. Workouts sync through your private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can read. Apple Health is optional. Export every set as CSV whenever you like.
