Set 2 · logging

One tap.Next set.

Weight and reps come filled in from last time, so most sets take a single tap. Kilos, pounds or stone, switched right from the weight field.

Set 3 · rest

Rest.It startsitself.

After every set a rest timer can start on its own, with a sound, an optional spoken cue and a notification when the app is in the background.

Set 4 · history

Beatlast time.

Your earlier sessions sit right under the entry fields, so you always know the number to beat.

Set 5 · privacy

Nobodyis watching.

No sign-up, no ads, no analytics. Workouts sync through your private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can read. We never see your training or your health data.

Set 6 · price

Pay once.Or never.

Logging, timers, history, iCloud sync, Apple Health and CSV export are free. Goodrep Pro is a one-time unlock for full charts, body charts and alternate app icons, shared with your family. No subscription, ever.
