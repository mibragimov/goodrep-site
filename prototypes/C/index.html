Four rounds · a session in Goodrep

Plan. Log. Look back.

  1. Build it once

    Pick from more than 400 exercises on a front-and-back body map, set targets like 3 × 8–10, pair supersets and give each exercise its own rest.

  2. One tap a set

    Weight and reps come filled in from last time, and your earlier sessions sit right under the fields, so you always know what to beat.

  3. Read the total

    The summary shows sets, reps, volume and time, with a front-and-back map of the muscles you trained. Share it as an image.

  4. Every day on record

    A calendar marks every training day, charts follow your lifts, and the Body tab keeps your weight, body fat and tape measurements.
