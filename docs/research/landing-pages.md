# Landing pages: Mobbin study for goodrep.fitness (round 1)

Research for the three landing-page prototypes in `prototypes/A`, `B` and `C` (`docs/design/landing-round1.md`).
Studied on Mobbin on 2026-10-30 with `search_sections` (web sections) and `search_screens` (web and iOS).

**Method and a caveat.** Mobbin's web library has no marketing site for most of the named fitness apps (searches for
the Strava, WHOOP, Oura, Peloton and Zwift homepages returned unrelated sites). For those apps the study uses their
**iOS welcome and onboarding screens**, which are their first sales pitch inside the app and follow the same rules as a
landing hero: one claim, one picture, one button. Fitness **websites** that Mobbin does have (ClassPass, Sweatpals,
Bevel, Superpower, Hers, Headspace, Nike After Dark Tour, the Squarespace and Framer fitness templates) are studied as
websites.

This is inspiration only. No copy, layout file, image or mark was taken from any reference, and no Mobbin image is
committed. Links go to Mobbin so the owner can open each one.

## What this means for Goodrep

1. **Numbers are our photography.** The best fitness references that are not stock photos (Hevy, Any Distance,
   Apple Fitness) lead with a set, a time or a total. Our screenshots already have "80 KG", "2,168", "1:30". Every
   variant puts a real number from the app at hero scale.
2. **Don't look like Ladder or Hers.** Lime on black alone is shared ground. What is ours: the **wide** expanded caps,
   the **slanted seven-segment G** (the gym's interval clock), the mono labels, and the honesty of a log with no
   account, no coach and no feed.
3. **No people, no stock, no gradients.** Volt forbids gradients and glows; the research shows people photos are the
   template (ClassPass, Strava, Centr, WHOOP). We have none and want none.
4. **Type at poster scale works on black** (Nike After Dark, TinyWins, Grail). One colour is enough.
5. **The privacy promise is the differentiator a community app cannot make.** Strava sells "150+ million people";
   we sell "nobody, not even us, sees your workouts". It deserves a whole screen, not a footer line.
6. **Footers** close with the name at full width plus the legal links (Sweatpals, KOBU): privacy, support and the
   support email sit there in every variant.
7. **One CTA state.** The app is not live, so every variant shows one honest "Coming to the App Store" state where the
   badge will go, and nothing that pretends to download.
