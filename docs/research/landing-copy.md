# Landing-page copy: what everyone says, and what Goodrep can say (copy round)

The owner chose variant C ("Gym clock") and asked for new words: "the wording needs a writer, rest timer is not the
wow feature". This study looks at the **words** of 25 product pages and 12 Mobbin screens, not their visuals (those were
studied in `landing-pages.md`). Live pages were fetched on 2026-10-30. The fetch tool passes pages through a
summariser, so quotes are close to verbatim but should be checked before anyone leans on one. Lines are quoted here
for reference only. None of them is reused on our page.

Not fetched: WHOOP (403 on two URLs; covered from Mobbin below), Linear (renders with JavaScript only, nothing to
read). Arc's homepage is now a sunset notice, so it is listed but is no use as a model.

## 5. Synthesis

### What every lifting app promises

1. **Log fast.** "Log every set in seconds", "Log a set faster than you can rack the bar", "Fastest workout tracker
   app", "just works". Every page claims speed; almost none shows *why* it is fast.
2. **See progress.** "Know exactly how strong you're getting", "See your progress as it happens", "Watch your strength
   climb", "See it all add up". A rising chart is the category's stock picture.
3. **Plans or coaching.** Fitbod, Ladder, Boostcamp, Alpha, Juggernaut, RP, Caliber, Liftin' all sell the program.
   Goodrep has no programs in v1 (SPEC §1.2), so this is not our fight.
4. **The crowd.** Ratings counts and user counts in the first screen (Hevy 17 million, Strava 100 million, Setgraph
   50 million sets). We have none before launch, and inventing any is out.

### What nobody says, and Goodrep can say truthfully

| Claim | Who else says it | Goodrep source |
|---|---|---|
| **Last time's numbers are already in the fields**, and earlier sessions sit under them | Liftin' hints ("Heavier next time"); Gravitus ("one tap from the set"). Nobody makes it the headline | metadata, Description: "Weight and reps are filled in from last time, so most sets take one tap"; "Your earlier sessions sit right under the entry fields, so you always know what to beat" (SPEC E4.2, E4.5) |
| **No account at all** | Alpha Progression only, as a "guest account" line in a feature list | metadata: "No sign-up, no ads, no analytics"; SPEC §1.1 "no accounts" |
| **No server of ours; data in your own iCloud** | Liftin' names iCloud sync. Bear says it best, outside fitness | metadata: "your private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can read"; App Privacy: "there is no server of ours" |
| **Pay once or never** | Nobody in strength. Hevy sells charts monthly, with a lifetime tier at the end | owner rule 2026-10-30; SPEC E11: one non-consumable, Family Sharing, no subscription |
| **CSV out and back in** | Strong ("Export your data any time."), Alpha ("CSV export") | metadata: "Export every logged set as a CSV file… and import it again on any device" (E6.5) |
| **Apple Health** | Strong, Alpha, Liftin' | metadata, "Works with Apple Health" (E8) |

### Three positioning angles for Goodrep

**1. The number to beat.** Progressive overload is the one thing every lifter does every session: match last time,
then beat it. Goodrep puts last time in the fields and lists the earlier sessions under them, so the target is on the
screen before the bar is. It is the screenshot we already have (80 kg × 8 filled in; yesterday 80 × 8 underneath).
- H1: **BEAT LAST TIME.**
- Sub: "Open an exercise and last session's weight and reps are already in the fields, with your earlier sets listed
  under them. Most sets take one tap."

**2. Your log, nobody else's.** Ownership as the headline: no account, no ads, no tracking, no server, CSV out.
Unique in the category, but it is a reason to trust, not a reason to pick up the phone mid-set.
- H1: **YOUR LOG. NOBODY ELSE'S.**
- Sub: "No account to make, no ads, no tracking. Every set lives on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, and leaves as
  CSV whenever you want."

**3. Free to lift, pay once.** Price model as the hook against subscription fatigue. True and rare, but it leads with
money, and the page may not show a price.
- H1: **NO SUBSCRIPTION.**
- Sub: "Logging, timers, history, sync and export are free. Pro is one purchase for progress charts and alternate
  icons, shared with your family."

Recommendation: angle 1 leads (hero and the screens), angle 2 is the scoreboard, angle 3 is the Pro section. Each
appears once, in that order, so the page goes from *why you'd open it* to *why you can trust it* to *what it costs*.
